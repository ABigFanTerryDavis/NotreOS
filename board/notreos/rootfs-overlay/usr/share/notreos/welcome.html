P4DE

Platform 4 Desktop Environment is NotreOS's graphical session. This is the minimal Ancient 0.0.1 edition.

Getting around

  • This browser is Dillo, launched on the X.org graphical session.
  • Switch to virtual terminal 2 for NotreShell.
  • NotreShell runs programs from PATH; try help, version, pwd, or ls.
  • The live system runs from the kernel's embedded initramfs. Changes in RAM are temporary.

Ancient 0.0.1 does not yet include a separate window manager, panel or settings application. Device support and installer tooling are intentionally limited.
